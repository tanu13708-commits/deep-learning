# 00 — To Learn

## Recommended Learning Order

1. **Neural Network Fundamentals** — neurons, layers, weights, biases, forward pass, loss.
2. **Activation Functions** — Sigmoid, Tanh, ReLU and variants.
3. **Backpropagation & Optimization** — chain rule, gradients, gradient descent, learning rate, optimizers.
4. **Loss Functions** — regression and classification losses.
5. **Training & Regularization** — epochs, batches, overfitting, dropout, normalization, early stopping, weight decay.
6. **CNNs** — convolution, filters, feature maps, pooling, transfer learning.
7. **Sequential Models** — RNNs, LSTM and GRU.
8. **Attention & Transformers** — Q/K/V, self-attention, multi-head attention, positional information.
9. **Transfer Learning** — feature extraction, freezing, fine-tuning, catastrophic forgetting.
10. **Practical Deep Learning** — pipelines, debugging, GPU memory, reproducibility, saving/loading and deployment.

## Interview Answer Pattern

**Definition → Intuition → Formula/architecture → Example → When/why it is useful**
